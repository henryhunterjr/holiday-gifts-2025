Questions bakers ask

Which grain should a new home miller start with?

Hard white wheat. It mills easily and bakes into a light, mild whole wheat loaf. Once they're comfortable, einkorn and spelt add flavor.

Can I bake with einkorn like regular flour?

Mostly. Einkorn absorbs less water and has weaker gluten, so use a little less liquid and expect a softer dough. The flavor is worth it.
