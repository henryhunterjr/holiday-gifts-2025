Questions bakers ask

Straight or curved lame: which should I gift?

A straight blade cuts clean, upright lines and decorative patterns. A curved blade sits at an angle and lifts a tall ear on a batard. If you don't know their style, a straight UFO is the safer first gift.

Are replacement blades easy to find?

Yes. Wire Monkey lames take standard double-edge razor blades, the kind sold at any drugstore, and the blade swaps without tools.

What's the best Wire Monkey gift under $30?

The Poco and the Arc are both compact, full working lames. See the current prices above.
