Questions bakers ask

Impact mill or stone mill: which should I gift?

Impact mills like the Classic and Impact are fast and make fine bread flour. Stone mills like the Harvest and Flourish run slower and cooler and give a classic stone-ground texture. For bread, either works. Speed favors impact, character favors stone.

Do I need a special mixer for whole grain dough?

Whole grain dough is heavy. A bottom-drive mixer like the Bosch Universal Plus or Artiste handles it without the strain that tilt-head mixers feel.
