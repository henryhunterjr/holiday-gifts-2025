Questions bakers ask

What is the best Brød & Taylor gift for a beginner sourdough baker?

Start with the High Capacity Baking Scale, the Sourdough Starter Jar, or the Boule Banneton Set. The scale moves a new baker from cups to grams, the jar makes starter rise easy to read, and the bannetons give round loaves clean shape and easy release. All three come in under $30.

Boule vs. Batard banneton: which should I gift?

A boule basket makes a round loaf and suits most everyday doughs. A batard basket makes an oval loaf that slices evenly for sandwiches and handles bigger doughs, around 900 g to 1.1 kg in the large size. If you don’t know their habits, the boule set is the safer first gift.

Why is the Folding Proofer worth the splurge?

Dough rises on temperature, and a cold kitchen makes timing a guess. The Folding Proofer holds the exact degree you set, so rise times stay predictable all winter. It folds flat for drawer storage and also works as a slow cooker.
