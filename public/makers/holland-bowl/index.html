Questions bakers ask

What size wooden bowl is best for bread dough?

The 15 inch. It holds a full batch with room to work the dough by hand. The 12 inch handles a single loaf.

How do I care for a wooden bowl?

Hand wash with warm water, dry it right away, and oil it now and then with a food-safe oil. Never soak it or put it in the dishwasher.
