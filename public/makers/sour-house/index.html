Questions bakers ask

What does Goldie actually do?

Goldie holds your starter at a steady warm temperature, so it peaks when you plan instead of when the kitchen allows. The included Cooling Puck slows it down when the room runs hot.

Goldie or DoughBed: which should I gift?

Goldie is for the starter. DoughBed is for the dough during bulk proof. If they have neither, start with Goldie or the Essential Bundle. The Baking On Your Schedule Bundle covers both.
