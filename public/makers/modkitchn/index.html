Questions bakers ask

What is a bread sling?

A silicone sling with handles. You set the dough on it and lower it into a hot Dutch oven without touching the pot. It replaces parchment and you reuse it every bake.

Round or oval sling?

Match the pot and the loaf. Round for a round Dutch oven and boules, oval for an oval pot and batards.

How long does bread stay fresh in the storage bags?

Longer than on the counter or in plastic. The liner manages moisture so the crust stays better and the crumb doesn't dry out as fast.
