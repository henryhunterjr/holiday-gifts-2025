Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026.

What stays on your device

Your saved gifts live in your own browser. We don’t see them unless you share your list link with someone.

Anonymous visit counts

We count page views and clicks on store links and codes so we know which gifts help people. Each visit gets a random ID. We don’t collect your name, email or IP address for this, and we skip it if your browser asks not to be tracked.

Giveaway entries

If you enter the weekly giveaway, we keep the name and email you give us to pick and contact a winner. We don’t sell it.

Store links

Links to partner stores are affiliate links. Those stores have their own privacy policies and handle checkout, payment and shipping.
