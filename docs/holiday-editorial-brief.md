# Holiday editorial redesign

Self-authored from Henry’s supplied direction and existing assets.

Direction: modern editorial, tactile, generous, warm. Evergreen, parchment and a measured cranberry accent. Authentic partner photography only. No new Classic launch material.

Journey and feeling: recognition (the baker’s hands and a real loaf); curiosity (a layered arrangement of useful gifts); possibility (mill and grain paired in one generous spread); confidence (Henry’s curated edit); agency (search, filter, Gift Finder and saved gifts); generosity (resources and a final invitation to bake).

Peak: “It’s the guide where the mill and the grain become a gift for a whole new baking ritual.” The partner spread has the largest combined product presentation.

Grammar: editorial collection with independent photographic planes in the hero, a paired partner spread, an open product grid, functional catalog and a quiet resource close. A continuous film, pinned sequence, horizontal rail, or cinematic scrub would interfere with shopping. No video or generated product assets are needed. Native CSS scroll timelines add different depth to the two hero photographs on larger screens. Touch and reduced-motion retain the composed static arrangement.

Signature: a two-part grain-to-gift spread, pairing a verified NutriMill Impact link and code with Grand Teton einkorn and a first-order offer, followed by Henry’s grain guide and challenge.

Evidence and limitations are recorded in the redesign QA report. This brief is authored, not an interview transcript. The cloud scroll skill’s supplementary reference resources could not be read by the current skill provider; its local doctor/engine were unavailable. Existing React architecture is retained.
